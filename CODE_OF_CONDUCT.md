# 📋 Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone in the Block Game community, regardless of:

- Age
- Body size
- Disability
- Ethnicity
- Gender identity and expression
- Level of experience
- Education
- Socio-economic status
- Nationality
- Personal appearance
- Race
- Religion
- Sexual identity and orientation
- Ability

This Code of Conduct applies to all community interactions including but not limited to:
- GitHub discussions and issues
- Pull requests and code reviews
- Comments and feedback
- Social media interactions
- In-person events
- Private communications

## Our Standards

### ✅ Examples of Behavior That Contributes to a Positive Environment

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Being patient with newcomers
- Offering help and guidance
- Celebrating others' achievements
- Admitting mistakes and learning from them
- Asking questions to understand rather than to criticize

### ❌ Examples of Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment in any form (sexual, racist, homophobic, etc.)
- Offensive comments related to protected characteristics
- Deliberate intimidation or threats
- Stalking or following behavior
- Unwanted physical contact or attention
- Posting or displaying sexually explicit material
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Spam or promotional content
- Sharing others' private information without consent
- Any form of discrimination
- Condescending or dismissive language
- Repeatedly arguing in bad faith

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will:

- Monitor community spaces for violations
- Respond promptly and fairly to reports
- Take appropriate action against violations
- Protect the privacy of incident reporters
- Communicate decisions transparently

- Reporter names will not be shared without permission

## Enforcement Actions

### Response Levels

Depending on the severity and context, community leaders may take actions including:

#### Level 1: Warning
- **For:** First-time minor violations, unintentional behavior
- **Action:** Private conversation and clear warning
- **Outcome:** Understanding the issue and commitment to change

#### Level 2: Temporary Restriction
- **For:** Repeated minor violations or single serious incident
- **Action:** Temporary muting or suspension from community
- **Duration:** 7-30 days depending on severity
- **Outcome:** Reflection period

#### Level 3: Permanent Ban
- **For:** Severe violations or repeated serious incidents
- **Action:** Permanent removal from community
- **Outcome:** User banned from all community spaces

### Appeal Process

- Users may appeal enforcement decisions within 30 days
- Appeal should include new information or context
- Appeals are reviewed by a different community leader
- Decision is final after appeal review

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

## Examples in Action

### Scenario 1: Disagreement on a Feature

**What Happened:**
Two contributors disagree on how to implement a feature.

**Positive Interaction:**
- Each listens to the other's perspective
- Both acknowledge valid points
- They discuss trade-offs respectfully
- They work together to find a compromise
- The discussion remains focused on the technical issue

**Negative Interaction:**
- Name-calling and personal attacks
- Dismissing opinions without consideration
- Making it personal rather than technical
- Refusing to acknowledge valid concerns
- Insulting the other person's intelligence

### Scenario 2: Newcomer Asking Questions

**What Happened:**
A new contributor asks a basic question that's been asked before.

**Positive Interaction:**
- Experienced members answer patiently
- They provide helpful resources
- They appreciate the question
- They encourage continued participation
- They remember being new once

**Negative Interaction:**
- Harsh responses or eye-rolling
- Pointing out the question was asked before
- Making the person feel stupid
- Discouraging future questions
- Creating a hostile environment

### Scenario 3: Receiving Criticism

**What Happened:**
Someone points out an issue with your contribution.

**Positive Interaction:**
- You listen to the feedback
- You ask clarifying questions
- You consider the points raised
- You thank them for the review
- You make improvements or explain your reasoning

**Negative Interaction:**
- Getting defensive immediately
- Attacking the reviewer's credibility
- Refusing to consider their points
- Making excuses without reflection
- Creating conflict
